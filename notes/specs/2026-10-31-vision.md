# The Renascitur History Engine: Vision

**Status:** r2, 2026-10-31. r1 was checked by an independent reviewer against the code and master spec; r2 corrects
what canon enforcement really is today (§5), reorders the work (§7), and states what exists (§3). A direction, not a
build plan: it says where the engine is heading and why, and later specs do the detail. Aaron's words set the
direction; the extrapolation in §4 is Claude's and is there to be corrected. Comment on the review queue (#150) to
overrule any of it.

**Standing:** the master spec's decisions stand until a later spec changes them, including its Canon and Heart rows.
This document says which way those specs should lean. Where it says "should", the thing is not built.

**References:** master spec `notes/specs/2026-10-21-history-engine-design.md`; tickets #128 (disease), #162 (pools,
provenance, `renasci graph`), #163 (marks), #131 (immortal agents).

## 1. What this is

**A world where nothing happens without leaving a trace, and the traces change what happens next.**

It is not a story generator. Stories are found in the world afterwards. The past is held in things as well as in
the log: a town's unburied dead, a sword's century of duels, a keeper scarred by years beside raw Lux Lapis. The
engine's output is source material: Aaron authors by watching, nudging and canonising.

**The founding example.** Famine does not lead to disease. People die, and the bodies may not be disposed of,
because of the scale of the task and the weakness of those left. That breeds sickness. No rule connects famine to
plague, and the same route should open after a sack, with nobody having written it. (Not yet: sacks kill no masses
today, see §7.)

## 2. Principles

1. **Emergence over scripting.** In new world mechanics, rules read conditions; they do not fire off other systems'
   events, and a system doesn't name the systems it influences. This is about mechanics: the scheduler's follow-ups
   (master §4.3) and existing direct calls, such as a death ending that person's ambitions, stay.
2. **Stocks and limits.** Prefer quantities that build and drain, with limited capacity to clear them. Most
   interesting collapses happen where a limit is reached.
3. **Shared currencies.** Labour, health, food, fear and faith connect systems: a town burying its dead isn't
   bringing in the harvest.
4. **No special cases.** A death is a death, whichever system caused it. (A goal: deaths take several paths today,
   see §7.)
5. **Every effect has a why.** Whatever emerges must trace back to its causes, at the grain the sim keeps. For the
   masses that is a settlement and a season, with a capped list of citations, as the master spec samples aggregates.
6. **Easy to extend.** Anything modular lives in a pool, and an extension in one file can add to it (#162).
7. **Replayable.** On one engine version, a recipe (with its extensions and canon) gives the same history. Ripples
   stay recorded interventions.
8. **Authored beats are allowed, and identifiable.** Reactions exist for deliberate story, not as the way the world's
   mechanics work, and the log shows which events came from one. Explicit graph links only describe (#162).

## 3. One grammar for everything

Five properties, available to any entity: a person, sword, town, river, guild, bloodline or god.

| Property | Meaning | Today |
|---|---|---|
| **Stocks** | What builds and drains: food, corpses, blood, fear, faith | Scattered settlement and person fields |
| **Marks** | What it has become | Planned (#163). Inborn `traits` don't change, but `skills`, `wound` and objects' `deeds` and `fame` are acquired state already |
| **Agency** | What it wants and can do | People; polities through rulers, or a council's weighted vote |
| **Beliefs** | What it holds, and what is said about it, true or not | Claims held by people, and by the masses as settlement shares (S6). Claims are values, not entities |
| **Provenance** | Why it is as it is | Events have causes. No general way to cite the events behind a condition; a few systems hand-carry ids (the relations ledger, a famine's harvest, deeds) |

The direction is to stop treating people as special, while they stay the heart of the stories. Generic systems act
on these properties, so a rule written once works on anything that has them. **Marks are built for any entity from
the start**, and **agency is built once**, as a capability shared by sentient objects and immortal beings (#163,
#131). **Stocks and exposures are one mechanism**, not two. Marks absorb what is already acquired state, or sit
beside it with a clear rule, and the existing `marks` field in `geo.py` is renamed or merged. Entities are what the
store has: bloodlines, rivers and guilds need a decision on what they are before they carry anything.

## 4. Where it leads

- **Places remember.** A battlefield is shunned for a century; salted land stays barren; a town that survived
  plague keeps its pits and its fear of strangers.
- **Bloodlines carry marks.** A family known for madness or luck; a house that can't escape its founder's deed.
- **Things change.** A blade that has seen enough death turns bloodthirsty, then sentient, and pushes its bearer. A
  smith becomes a master. A keeper is deformed by what they tended.
- **Hardship becomes culture.** A town that starved keeps a fasting festival. Plague becomes burial rites, which
  really do reduce plague. Custom hardens into law, and people forget why.
- **Knowledge can be lost.** A technique lives in practitioners, so plague can kill a craft, and a later age finds a
  ruined forge it can't reproduce.
- **Land has a past.** Forests are cut, mines run out, rivers shift, and those changes drive migration and war.
- **Things outlive their ages.** A First Age blade passes through every age, and the Fourth Age's myth of it is wrong
  in ways that can be traced.
- **Faith may make gods.** Enough belief in a saint or a sword might create something real. This conflicts with the
  S11 default that the sim makes no new gods: see §8.
- **The loop closes.** A discovery is canonised, a campaign is played in that world, and what the players did goes
  back in as ripples, to become the next century's legends. (The sim stops at 4A 256 today: see §8.)

**The deepest asset is the gap between what happened and what is believed.** The engine holds the truth, the lie and
the drift between them, each with a recorded cause. A myth that can be walked back to the real, unheroic event is
what could make this feel Tolkien-deep.

## 5. Canon: an open question

Holding fixed history in a world this free may become very hard. **This is deliberately not decided now**, and the
project may pivot.

**Where it really stands (measured 2026-10-31):**
- **The real canon isn't pinned.** Casting the actual vault for the Fourth Age gives no pins, most likely because no
  note has an `id:` yet (#60). Today's Fourth Age runs are effectively pinless, so the sim has never been tested
  against real canon.
- **The mechanism is proven only on a synthetic line.** The test of eight rulers reigning in order uses a made-up
  house in the `kin` world, on two seeds. In a three-seed check, 9 of its 14 born and reign pins were forced.
- **There is no steering.** The master spec's soft steering isn't built and no ticket covers it. Without it, a pin
  is either observed or forced.

So how hard canon is remains unknown, and the first real measurement is cheap to get and worth having early. The
pressure from the layers in §4 is a prediction, not an observation.

Directions to explore later:

| Direction | Canon is | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| **Hold the line** | Fixed points the sim must reach, by steering and pins | Strain rises with every system |
| **Soft canon** | Attractors: likely outcomes, with tolerances | Canon can bend or fail |
| **Canon as a start** | Conditions at a date, with the sim running forward from them | The past is not simulated, or is thin |
| **One branch of many** | One timeline; the others are what-ifs | Less emphasis on a single history |
| **Canon from the sim** | Adopted from what emerged, by seed and by choice | The vault's existing canon must yield |

**Until then, the engine must keep the choice open:**
- pins stay a separate layer that nothing else depends on, and a recipe can cast canon without pinning it (today
  casting and pinning are coupled);
- no system assumes a canon outcome will happen;
- strain is measured: the master spec's guards, steers, forces and violations, plus divergence from the same seed run
  without pins.

Running with no pins stays a supported, tested configuration. It is also the cheapest way to see what the engine
wants to do.

## 6. What will be hard

- **Legibility.** A bigger risk than the simulation itself. With everything interacting, output can turn to mush,
  so finding the story matters as much as producing it. The Observatory is half the product, and provenance is what
  lets it explain anything.
- **Deep time.** Coarse steps must keep stocks and marks meaningful across millennia, or deep time loses its texture.
  Each stock needs a coarse rule that a test compares with fine time, within a stated tolerance.
- **Runaway loops.** Systems that feed themselves can empty towns or the world. Mechanisms are tested with
  counterfactuals across seeds, not by asserting a toll.
- **Scale.** Universal properties on every entity cost time and memory. Only what has a history carries one: an absent
  field means the default, and provenance stays within the run size budget.
- **Agency beyond people.** Drives, ambitions and planning assume a person. Sentience is a real engine change.

## 7. Working order

Rough, and meant to blur. Each step that creates something to see gets its Observatory view with it, as the master
spec's two tracks require.

1. **Pools** and extensions (#162).
2. **Provenance:** a general way to cite the events behind a condition, generalising the relations ledger.
3. **One death path:** natural and hunger deaths of the masses and notables' deaths meet at one point, and sacks kill
   masses. Burial depends on both.
4. **Labour** as a shared currency within a season. Burial must compete with the harvest, or the founding example
   isn't real, and adding this later would rework burial.
5. **Stocks** as one mechanism, starting with `corpses`, then **burial and disease** (#128): the first emergent chain
   built on purpose.
6. **Marks on any entity** (#163), on stocks.
7. **Measure canon:** give the vault its ids (#60, Aaron's run), cast it, and record the real strain (§5). Can happen
   any time; earlier is better.
8. **`renasci graph`**, with implicit and explicit links, and emergent routes first-class (#162).
9. **Agency as a capability**, including `sentient`, which #163 leaves to this step. S11's beings build on it, so #131
   waits for it rather than building a second path.
10. **Culture and knowledge**, a design pass of their own after disease: living traditions, and techniques that can
    be lost. Fear and faith as stocks belong here.
11. **Land** and **memory of places**, using marks and stocks.

The rest of the backlog (night, the First Age, deep time) continues alongside, and follows the principles in §2.

**How we'd know it's working:**
- a chain nobody wrote appears in a run, and traces back, end to end, through recorded causes;
- removing one mechanism, by its off switch, changes outcomes across seeds as a stated prediction says it should;
- a new mark or action takes one file and no engine edit;
- the Observatory surfaces something Aaron finds worth canonising, without being told where to look.

## 8. Open questions

1. **Canon** (§5): which direction, and when to decide. Default: keep all of them open, and measure first.
2. **Steering:** build it, or let the canon decision settle whether it's needed? Default: wait for the measurement.
3. **New gods:** may enough faith create a real one? Default: not yet; S11's default stands until culture is designed.
4. **Player campaigns as input:** is that loop wanted, and how formal should it be? Default: ripples, by hand.
5. **After the present:** the master spec stops the sim at 4A 256, so play has no simulated future to feed. Backdate
   ripples, or extend the calendar? Default: decide with question 4.
6. **People as the heart:** does a world of agents beyond people change the master spec's Heart row? Default: no.
