## 1. Scope and shape

The First Age is ten thousand years: the engine's long-run test. It is mostly coarse (decades at a step), with
**focus windows** around each canon event, so the age's turning points are lived at full grain and its quiet
millennia cost little.

**A ruleset of its own** (`first_age`): its own world data (races including the Elder Races and Solarans, a
draft place graph, a technology tree, a pantheon), its own systems (technology, World Trees, cosmic events,
myth), and S11 at `presence: present`.

**Canon holds; story is generated.** The dated notes are pins (§2). The colonies, inventions, schisms and
champions between them are generated, and stay draft until promoted.

**M5 scope:** the canon pins, the Endless Sun's chain (technology, projects, schisms, gods), myths, and the
budget. **Stretch** (M5 if time allows, else M6): the stars as a void graph (#145), technology diffusion and
loss (#141's second half), Elder realms as full polities.

## 2. Canon audit and pins (#140)

- **What S2 and the calendar gain first:**
  - a **1A age** in the calendar (`last_year = 10000`);
  - a per-age **`circa_years`** tolerance in `vault.toml` (±50 before 1A 5000, ±20 after; S2 parses absolute
    dates as exact years today);
  - **year 0** (the Creation) marked reference-only, since 1A starts at 1;
  - the event notes' **frontmatter** (`engine:` type and `participants:` per note): a vault task for Aaron,
    listed by the audit. S2 makes no pin for a note without it.
- Each note with frontmatter becomes an **event pin** with a window (its date ± the age's `circa_years`), its
  type (`project.completed` for the Endless Sun, `polity.fallen` for Solara, `deity.imprisoned` for Syuul…)
  and roles (bound to cast entities), plus **ordering pins** where the notes imply sequence.
- **Forcers** make pinned deeds happen in their windows if the world hasn't, and each forced deed is classified
  (strain visible, as S2's).
- **The audit** (`renasci vault check --age first`): every note mapped to a pin or marked reference-only, with
  gaps listed (no frontmatter, no entity for an actor).

## 3. Civilisation-scale polities (#142)

- Solara and its colonies, and later Runehart, are S8 polities run at coarse LOD (S8 Part A's yearly war draw,
  relations, treaties) on a **draft First Age place graph** (a handful of great cities and regions; no map
  exists for this age).
- **Long lives:** S3 races gain `band_years` (five by default; fifty or a hundred for the Elder Races and
  Solarans), so a two-thousand-year life gets its own mortality and fertility curve rather than a flat "95+".
  Population columns follow the race's bands. S11's `mortal: false` stays for true immortals.
- **Notables at low density:** a few hundred per great city, so dynasties span centuries; the masses as numbers.
- **Schisms:** S8's Secede and Usurp at civilisation scale, which give the Collapse of Solara pin its
  generated causes.

## 4. Solaran technology (#141)

- **A technology tree** (world data; #149's vocabulary): `techs: {id: {name, field, cost, requires, effects}}`.
  Effects are hooks other systems read: yields, a route kind, a project.
- **Research:** scholars (an S9 job) and a polity's programmes accumulate progress per field each year. A tech
  is realised by a keyed draw when its prerequisites hold and progress passes its cost (`tech.realised`,
  crediting the scholars and the polity).
- **Projects:** a great work (the Endless Sun) is a multi-decade project; a polity commits resources each year,
  and it completes (`project.completed`) or fails. Its fall is a cosmic event (§6).
- **New S7 classes** (eligible only in the `first_age` ruleset's library, so other worlds are untouched):
  ambitions `Discover` (a scholar's) and `Build` (a ruler's great work), and the actions they plan (`Research`,
  `Commission`).
- **Stretch:** diffusion along routes to neighbours, and loss when a polity falls (`tech.lost`, a later age's
  rediscovery).

## 5. World Trees and the Elder Races (#143)

- **World Trees** are entities (`kind: world_tree`) at places. They take root (pinned, 1A 1000), bless races (a
  trait boost for those living near), anchor realms (a polity seated by one gains standing), and can be harmed
  (research, 1A 5500; corruption, Ishna).
- **The Elder Races** (blessed, 1A 500): long-lived races (§3) bonded to a Tree. A realm whose Tree fails
  weakens.

## 6. Elemental and cosmic events (#144)

- A catalogue of cosmic-scale **structural events** built on S10's mechanisms (no new ripple kinds):
  conjunctions, elemental upheavals (Muradin's communion is a deity-scale event with S11), a great work's fall.
  Their effects go through existing paths: S4 map changes, S10's `population.kill`, S8 falls, a Tree harmed.
- Pinned ones are forced by their pins; others draw at catalogue rates per century.

## 7. The stars: an abstract off-world realm (#145, stretch)

- A **place graph** apart from the world's map: star systems as places, linked by `void` routes that need the
  `void` tech. Solaran colonies sit on them (S8 polities).
- **Voyages** are S4 journeys by `void` routes: long, dangerous, some lost.
- The Solarans reaching the stars (pinned, 1A 2000) is the realisation of `void` travel and the first colony.
  Until this ships, that pin is met by a `tech.realised` event alone.

## 8. Myth formation (#146)

- **A carrier:** each settlement's `shares` (S6) keep myths, not notables' beliefs, which fade (`decayed`,
  `BELIEF_CAP`) and die with their holders. A **myth** is a claim of a new `myth(event, as)` predicate, where
  `as` is a story shape (a hero's deed, a god's wrath, a betrayal). It is seeded into the towns' shares by
  salient events, its share decaying slowly.
- **Drift by century:** a `myths` system, which has a coarse fallback, applies S6's drift to each surviving
  myth once a century: names change, a house becomes a people, a god takes the credit. It records what each
  myth drifted from.
- **A judge:** `analysis.truth` gains `myth`. It is "true" if `as` matches the event's recorded shape (its
  actors and outcome), "half" if it names the right event with the wrong actors, and "false" otherwise.
- **Handoff seeds:** at the age's end, each culture's surviving myths are written to `myths.jsonl`, the claim
  seeds later ages load (#136).

## 9. Temporal LOD at a 10,000-year scale

- **Coarse by default** (decade steps).
- **Focus windows** are computed before the run from the pins: each window runs from 25 years before its pin's
  window opens to the end of that window, so a canon deed and its forcer both fall in fine time. Windows are
  part of the recipe's resolution policy (hashed, as today).
- **Opening a window at run time** (on a great war, a god manifesting, a project nearing completion) is a new S1
  feature: a recorded `lod.focus_open` that cuts the current step short and is replayed from the event log, not
  the recipe. It is a separate S1 ticket and a stretch goal; S11's `Manifest` trigger waits for it.
- **The coarse fallbacks still to write:** `people.life`, `people.population`, `people.births`, beliefs'
  gossip and shares, S11's `divine` and `faith`, and S14's own systems. Life and population are seasonal today
  (40,000 seasons over the age), so their fallbacks are the budget's heart.
- **Budget:** the full 1A 1–10000 run within the overnight budget on the reference machine, with profiling
  (#139) as plan step 1.

## 10. Exit criteria (with the master spec's M5 demo)

- **Canon holds or is classified:** every First Age pin met or classified, over a fixed seed set.
- **The golden (#147),** 1A 1–10000 on a chosen seed:
  - the Endless Sun's `project.completed` falls in its window and **isn't forced**: the event carries no forcer
    cause, and its cause chain reaches a generated `tech.realised` (by named scholars) within 5 hops;
  - its fall's chain reaches a generated, non-pin event (a schism, a corruption, a god's act) within 5 hops;
  - S11's pinned divine deeds hold, their chains running through divine agents' actions.
- **Generated lore is promotable:** a generated colony's saga and a named invention promote through
  `renasci promote` into a draft vault note.
- **A myth survives:** at least one First Age event yields a `myth` seed in `myths.jsonl` that the judge reads
  as "false" or "half".
- **Budget:** the full age within the overnight budget.
