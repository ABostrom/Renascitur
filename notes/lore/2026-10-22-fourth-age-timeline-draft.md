# Fourth Age timeline: draft

Each row is tagged with where it comes from:

- **[vault]**: stated in a vault note, with the note cited.
- **[derived]**: follows from vault facts plus a little arithmetic.
- **[proposed]**: my invention to fill a gap. Change it freely.

When you've corrected this note, the engine turns it into pins (S2): a *succession* pin for the
Emperors, and *event*/*ordering* pins with windows for the rest. Rows you leave vague stay as windows,
and the sim fills in the exact year.

**What this implies:**

- The campaign present is about **4A 256**, not 4A 250.
- **Octavian is the living, reigning Emperor.**
- Seven Emperors reigned across about 4A 10 to the present, which averages about 31 years each. That
  fits Leonin with a human-like lifespan.

The vault doesn't say whether succession passes **father to son**. Each spouse is a different
person, so this is your call. The options are:

- **(a)** a dynasty, with each Emperor the child of the last
- **(b)** elective or adoptive, chosen by the Imperial Council of the Great Houses (Roman-style)
- **(c)** a mix

(b) gives the sim the most room for succession schemes, and the Great Houses would have real stakes.

## Questions for you

1. **Present day:** is it **4A 256** (the Deep Line inscription), or is "4th Era 256" a different
   count from the Fourth Age?
2. **Is Octavian alive and reigning now?** The Tomb has no chamber for him.
3. **Succession:** dynasty, Council election, or a mix? (See above. (b) gives the most story.)
4. **Unimus:** is a ~38-year reign ending in ascension at about 4A 48 right, or did he reign much
   longer? His godhood might also have extended his life.
5. **The Great Houses:** did they exist *before* the Empire, for example as survivor clans that
   pledged to Unimus? Or were they formed by imperial charter, as proposed under Tertia?
6. **The Vialux and the Lux Lapis walls:** when were they built? I've proposed walls from Unimus's
   time and a Vialux expanded under Octavian.
7. **The Tomb of Emperors:** is it Septimus's project? Is Unimus's chamber a cenotaph, or does it
   hold a body (or an avatar; the note ends with "Avatar of Unimus")?
8. **Anything missing:** wars with other realms, plagues, a House destroyed or raised, a failed
   usurpation? Each one becomes an event pin.
